## Atmosphere Prompts

Opening: Six-second cinematic establishing shot, landscape 16:9. A single founder's tidy desk with one open laptop in a modern Singapore office, restrained navy and cool-blue accents, warm natural light. Slow controlled push-in. Laptop screen abstract and unreadable. No lettering, logos, speech or invented software interfaces. Leave space for editor-added titles.

Closing: Five-second cinematic closing shot, landscape 16:9. Calm office at dusk, one laptop on a tidy desk and a softly visible skyline. Restrained navy and blue with warm highlights. Gentle pullback. No text, logos, speech, mock software screens or holograms.

## Edit and Verification

Preserve originals. Redact personal addresses, unrelated inbox material, account IDs and credentials on the full source frame before fitting it into the output canvas. Never crop away source evidence. Keep labels outside important controls and keep the Send action readable. Use holds and hard cuts rather than repeated clicks. Mute unreviewed original audio. Record one consistent voiceover and measure its duration against the timeline; generated atmosphere is not proof of business functionality.

Before submission, decode the export, inspect duration and resolution, check transitions and Send timing, review privacy throughout and listen to the complete audio. A portable fit utility alone does not perform or certify those editorial checks. No new media generation or publication is requested by this document.
